# Tangent trademark policy

The code in this repository is MIT licensed. That licence covers the code, not
the name. The name Tangent, as used for this software, and the Tangent
application icon are trademarks of Matthew Gantenbein.

The aim of this policy is simple: nobody should be confused about what is the
official Tangent and what is not, and nobody should pass modified software off
as ours. It is modeled on the policies of projects like Rust and Firefox.

## You may, without asking

- Use the name Tangent to refer to this software, truthfully, in articles,
  videos, courses, package descriptions and comparisons.
- Distribute unmodified official builds, including bundling them with hardware
  or other software, provided you do not charge for Tangent itself and you
  link back to the official source.
- State compatibility, for example "works with Tangent" or "plugin for
  Tangent", as long as your product name does not begin with Tangent and no
  official endorsement is implied.
- Build the unmodified source for yourself, for your users, or for a software
  distribution, and call the result Tangent.

## You may not, without written permission

- Use Tangent, or a confusingly similar name, as the name of a modified
  version, a fork, or a derived product. Forks are welcome and the MIT licence
  guarantees that right; pick your own name and state that your work is
  derived from Tangent.
- Use the name or icon in a way that suggests your product, build, or service
  is official or endorsed.
- Sell keys, licences, or support under the Tangent name.
- Register Tangent, or a confusingly similar mark, as a trademark, company
  name, domain name, or social media handle for software or related services.
